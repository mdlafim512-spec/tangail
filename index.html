Tourist Spots in Tangail

The 201 Dome Mosque is located in South Pathalia village,Gopalpur Upazila, Tangail, Bangladesh. It is famous for its beautiful Islamic architecture, numerous domes, andtall minarets. The mosque is an important attraction for visitors interested in religion, culture, and architecture.

DC Lake, Tangail is a popular recreational spot known for its peaceful atmosphere and scenic surroundings. It offers visitors a relaxing place to enjoy the beauty of nature, spend quality time with family and friends, and capture beautiful photographs. Its calm environment makes it an attractive destination for local visitors seeking a break from busy daily life.

Mohera Zamindar Bari is a famous historical landmark located in Mirzapur, Tangail, Bangladesh. Built during the British colonial period, this magnificent estate is known for its beautiful European-style architecture, colorful buildings, spacious courtyards, and well-maintained green gardens. The complex features several impressive buildings, ponds, and scenic surroundings that attract tourists and history lovers. Today, Mohera Zamindar Bari stands as an important symbol of Bangladesh's rich history, architectural beauty, and cultural heritage.

Basulia Charpa Bil is a scenic wetland area in Tangail, Bangladesh, known for its natural beauty and peaceful rural surroundings. During the rainy season, the area fills with water, creating a beautiful landscape that attracts nature lovers. The bil reflects the charm of rural Bangladesh, with its aquatic plants, fish, and surrounding greenery. It is a wonderful place to appreciate nature and experience the peaceful beauty of the countryside.
